Before you dig

Good to know

When do I get my rank?+
Ranks are delivered automatically the moment your payment clears — no restart needed. If you're offline, it applies the next time you join.
Do ranks stack, or do I need to buy each one?+
Each rank includes every perk from the tiers above it. Buying Legend gives you Stone, Iron, Gold, and Diamond perks too — you only ever pay for the tier you want.
What payment methods are supported?+
Checkout is handled securely by Tebex, so you can pay by card, PayPal, and several other regional methods depending on your country.
Can I get a refund?+
Refund requests go through Tebex's standard policy. Reach out on Discord if something didn't apply correctly and we'll sort it out.
